# Confirm members

A new member signs in and links Battle.net, then the site confirms them automatically once
they're in the guild in game. Confirming opens the rest of the guild's Discord.

## Confirm someone on the roster

::::{note}
Members who link Battle.net and show up on the in-game guild roster get confirmed this way on
their own, usually within 30 minutes of the next roster sync (right away if they're already on
the roster when they link). You only need to confirm someone by hand if they haven't linked
Battle.net, their character isn't on the guild roster yet, or they won't sign in at all (see
below).
::::

Confirm from whichever place is handy:

1. **On the website:** go to the [Officers page](https://sidequest-guild.com/officers/),
   find the member, and confirm them.
2. **In game:** open the addon's Console, select the **Roster** tab, and confirm them there.

Confirming gives them the **New Arrival** Discord role and opens the guild channels.

::::{warning}
Removing someone's confirmation is always a manual step; nothing removes **New Arrival**
automatically. Once you remove it, auto-confirm skips that member until a Quest Master confirms
them again, even if they're still on the in-game guild roster.
::::

## Confirm someone who never signs in

Some members join the Discord and never sign in on the site. A Quest Master can still confirm
them:

1. Right-click (or long-press on mobile) their name in the member list.
2. Select **Apps**, then **Confirm in guild**.

This creates their site profile from their Discord name and avatar, then confirms them right
away. They get **New Arrival**, but never **Signed the Ledger**, since they haven't signed in.

## Approve a sign-in in Discord

The first time anyone signs in, the bot posts a notice in an officers-only channel with their
name, username, avatar and BattleTag (if linked).

1. Open the notice in the sign-ins channel.
2. Select **Approve**.

This does the same thing as confirming them on the website or in the addon; whichever you use
first updates the notice.

## Recruiting

Applicants and trials show up on the Officers page's **Recruiting** tab, and on the addon
Console's **Recruits** tab in game.

1. Open the applicant's entry.
2. Vote yes, no, or wait.
3. Add a note if you have context other officers should see.

Recruit notes and votes are officer-only; applicants never see them. Officers also set which
roles and classes the guild is recruiting under the **Recruiting** tab, so the homepage and the
welcome message show what's needed.
