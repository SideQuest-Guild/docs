# Confirm members

A new member signs in, links Battle.net, then waits for a Quest Master to confirm them in game.
Confirming opens the rest of the guild's Discord.

## Confirm someone on the roster

Confirm from whichever place is handy:

1. **On the website:** go to the [Officers page](https://sidequest-guild.com/officers/),
   find the member, and confirm them.
2. **In game:** open the addon's Console, select the **Roster** tab, and confirm them there.

Confirming gives them the **New Arrival** Discord role and opens the guild channels.

## Approve a sign-in in Discord

The first time anyone signs in, the bot posts a notice in an officers-only channel with their
name, username, avatar and BattleTag (if linked).

1. Open the notice in the sign-ins channel.
2. Select **Approve**.

This does the same thing as confirming them on the website or in the addon; whichever you use
first updates the notice.

## Recruiting

Applicants and trials show up on the Officers page's **Recruiting** tab, and on the addon
Console's **Recruits** tab in game.

1. Open the applicant's entry.
2. Vote yes, no, or wait.
3. Add a note if you have context other officers should see.

Recruit notes and votes are officer-only; applicants never see them. Officers also set which
roles and classes the guild is recruiting under the **Recruiting** tab, so the homepage and the
welcome message show what's needed.
