# Members

## Joining

Go to [sidequest-guild.com](https://sidequest-guild.com) and sign in with Discord. That gives
you the *Signed the Ledger* role.

Next, link your Battle.net account from your [account page](https://sidequest-guild.com/account/).
That gives you *Battle-Tagged*.

Once an officer confirms you're in the guild in game, you get *New Arrival*, and the rest of
the Discord channels open up. Until then you can see only the sign-in channel.

## Signing up for events

Every event has a sign-up on the website's [events page](https://sidequest-guild.com/events/)
and a matching thread in Discord's events forum, with **Going**, **Maybe**, and **Can't**
buttons. Use whichever is handy. Signing up in one place updates the other.

## The addon

The in-game addon shows the roster, who can craft what, upcoming raids, and your points. You
can also sign up for raids and ask for standby from inside the game. Install it from
[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1773662) or
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/SideQuest-Guild/addon/releases), then type `/sq` to open
it. See the [addon's developer page](developers/addon.md) for what each tab does.

## Points

The guild tracks two kinds of points.

**Renown** is for the [Hall of Heroes](https://sidequest-guild.com/heroes/). You earn it by
coming to events; raids earn the most. It unlocks badges and shows how active you've been.

**[SQL](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/z5p-aMyfT98)** (SideQuest Loot, said "squeel") decides
who wins loot in a raid. The addon tracks it, and the website shows it on the
[ledger page](https://sidequest-guild.com/ledger/), along with the full rules and prices. The
[raids page](https://sidequest-guild.com/raids/) explains how a raid night runs.
