# Members

This guide is for SideQuest guild members. It covers joining, events, the addon, points, your
account and Discord. Pick a page below for your task.

- **[Join the guild](join.md)**: sign in, link Battle.net, and learn the roles and ranks.
- **[Find and join events](events.md)**: sign up for raids, dungeons and other nights.
- **[Use the addon](addon.md)**: install it and read its tabs in game.
- **[Understand your points](points.md)**: Renown, SQL loot points, and your PR.
- **[Manage your account](account.md)**: link Battle.net, get a calendar link, mark away dates.
- **[Discord channels and roles](discord.md)**: find your way around the server.

:::{note}
Officer or Quest Master? See the [officers' guide](../officers/index.md) instead.
:::
