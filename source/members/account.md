# Manage your account

Your [account page](https://sidequest-guild.com/account/) links Battle.net, makes you a calendar
link, marks your away dates, and deletes your account.

:::{figure} ../_static/shots/account-chambers.webp
:alt: The account page's Calendar, Companion and Away sections.
:width: 100%

The account page's chambers: Calendar, Companion and Away.
:::

## Link or unlink Battle.net

1. Go to your [account page](https://sidequest-guild.com/account/).
2. Select **Link Battle.net** and sign in.
3. To remove it later, select **Unlink Battle.net**.

:::{warning}
Unlinking erases your Battle.net account ID and BattleTag right away. Officers can no longer
match you to your characters until you link again.
:::

## Get a calendar link

1. On your account page, find **Calendar**.
2. Select **Add to Google Calendar** or **Add to Apple Calendar**, or copy the link and add it
   to another calendar app yourself.

New events you've signed up for show up on their own from then on.

:::{note}
Anyone holding your calendar link can see the guild's events and your sign-ups. Keep it to
yourself.
:::

### Make a new calendar link

1. On your account page, select **Make a new link**.

The old link stops working right away.

## Mark away dates

1. On your account page, find **Away**.
2. Enter a **From** and **To** date, and an optional note.
3. Select **Add**.

While a range is active, raid leads see you're away on any event in that range, and you get the
**Away on a Quest** Discord role.

## Delete your account

:::{warning}
Deleting your account erases your SideQuest profile, every event sign-up and your Discord chat
XP, and signs you out. It also removes you from the guild on Discord: you lose your roles and
the guild channels disappear until you sign in again and a Quest Master confirms you.
:::

1. On your account page, scroll to **Danger Zone**.
2. Select **Delete my account**.
3. Select **Yes, delete my account** to confirm.

Moderation records in Discord (warnings, timeouts, bans) stay after you delete your account; ask
an officer about those. See the [privacy page](https://sidequest-guild.com/privacy/) for the
full list of what the site and bot keep, and what deleting removes.
