# Make gold with the market report

The market report tells you what sells on our auction house. Use it to choose what to gather
and what to craft.

You can read it in two places:

- **Discord:** `#auction-house` gets a new report every day at 10:00 Central.
- **The site:** [sidequest-guild.com/market](https://sidequest-guild.com/market/) has the full
  report. Sign in first. Only guild members can see it.

## Where the numbers come from

The numbers come from one Alliance auction house, the one most of the guild uses. Prices on
your house can be a little different.

- **Sells for** is what one item sold for in the last two weeks. It is never more than the
  lowest listing now, because you must match that listing to sell today.
- **Sold a day** is how many sold each day, on average. A high number means your stack sells
  fast.
- **Change** compares the newest day with the week before it. "Steady" means less than 1%.

## Find something to gather

1. Open the **Gather** section.
2. Select your profession: Herbalism, Mining, Skinning, Fishing, or Cloth. Cloth drops from
   humanoid monsters, so any character can farm it.
3. Select your level band: 1-24, 25-44, or 45-60.
4. Read the first rows. The best item for gold is at the top.
5. Read **Where** for the zones and their levels, and **Skill** for the gathering skill you
   need.

In Discord, each profession shows the best item for each level band.

:::{tip}
A cheap item that sells thousands a day can make more gold an hour than a rare herb. You
gather more of it, and it sells at once.
:::

## Find something to craft

1. Open the **Craft** section.
2. Select your profession.
3. Read **Profit a craft**. This is what one craft makes after the auction house cut, when you
   buy every material on the auction house or from a vendor.
4. Select the **Materials** price to see each material, how many you need, and where it is
   cheapest.
5. Check **Sold a day** before you make many. A craft that sells once a day can sit on the
   auction house for a week.

Some crafts have a cooldown, such as transmutes and Mooncloth. The report shows "Once a day"
or "Once every 4 days" under their name. You can make only one in that time.

If you gather the materials yourself, your profit is higher than the report shows.

## Today and this week

The site has two views:

- **Today** compares the newest day with the week before.
- **This week** compares the last seven days with the seven days before them. Use it to see
  slow changes, for example a herb that gets more expensive each day.

## Movers

**Movers** lists the materials and crafts whose price went up or down the most. A big rise can
mean that people need it for a new raid or quest. A big drop can mean that many people farm it
now.

## When the report is old

If the auction house data is more than a day and a half old, the site shows a warning and the
bot does not post that day.
