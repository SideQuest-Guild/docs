# Understand your points

SideQuest tracks two kinds of points: **Renown**, for showing up, and **SQL**, for who wins
loot.

## Renown

Renown is for the [Hall of Heroes](https://sidequest-guild.com/heroes/). You earn it by coming
to events: a raid is worth the most, then dungeons, then leveling and PvP. It unlocks badges and
shows how active you've been this season, this month, and all time.

:::{note}
In the API and the code, Renown is still called `ep`. On the site and in this guide, "Renown" is
the word members see.
:::

## SQL loot points

[SQL](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/z5p-aMyfT98) (SideQuest Loot, said "squeel") decides who
wins loot in a raid. It has two parts:

- **EP** (effort points): you earn these for raiding. You get points for a boss kill, for the
  raid's first wipe on a boss it hasn't killed yet, for being there at the start, and for
  staying to the end. Standby earns the same as being in the raid.
- **GP** (gear points): you spend these when you win an item. Everyone starts at 100 GP.

Your **PR** (priority) is your EP divided by your GP. When several people want an item for their
main spec, the highest PR wins it. Both EP and GP shrink a little each week, so recent raids
count for more than old ones.

:::{note}
The full rules and current prices are on the [ledger page](https://sidequest-guild.com/ledger/).
The [raids page](https://sidequest-guild.com/raids/) explains how a raid night runs, including
bidding.
:::

## Read your PR

1. Go to the [ledger page](https://sidequest-guild.com/ledger/) or open the addon's **Ledger**
   tab. Every confirmed guild member is listed, even before their first raid.
2. Find your character.
3. Check your EP, GP and PR. On the site, select **Breakdown** on your row to see each EP award
   and GP charge, and what it counts for today after decay.

A higher PR means you're more likely to win an item you bid on for your main spec.

## Plan ahead

The ledger page's **Calculator** shows what your EP, GP and PR will be after the next weekly
reset and raid. Pick yourself, or type your own EP and GP, then set the raid (on time, full
night, boss kills) and any loot you expect to win. It shows where that PR would rank. Select
**Compare with someone else** to put a second person beside you and see who would win a
contested main-spec drop. See [Loot: what happens when...](loot-scenarios.md) for how each kind
of drop is decided.
