# Discord channels and roles

The SideQuest Discord opens up as you join: sign in, link Battle.net, then get confirmed in
game. See [Join the guild](join.md) for the steps.

## Roles you'll hold

See [Roles and ranks](roles.md) for every role the server uses, how you get it, and what it
means. `#roles`, just below `#addons`, shows the same list in Discord.

## Addons

`#addons`, just below `#rules`, lists the addons raiders must run on raid nights and the ones
the guild suggests. The bot keeps it the same as the
[addons page](https://sidequest-guild.com/addons/), so read either one.

## The welcome DM and Start here

The first time you sign in, the bot sends you a welcome DM. If you miss it, select the pinned
**Start here** button in `#guest-chat` to see it again.

## Chat levels

Each message you send earns 15 to 25 XP, at most once a minute. Levels run from **Lv 5 ·
Wayfarer** up to **Lv 50 · Legend**, and the matching role is handed out on its own as you level
up, replacing the one before it. See [Roles and ranks](roles.md) for the full role list.

1. Type `/rank` in any channel to see your own level.
2. Type `/rank @someone` to see theirs.
3. Type `/leaderboard` to see the top of the server.

## Look things up

Type any of these in a channel. Start typing a name and Discord suggests matches. Item, quest,
beast and achievement answers have buttons for the site's page and the Wowhead page; `/who` has
an Armory button.

- `/item thunderfury` shows an item's tooltip.
- `/quest call of water` shows a quest: its zone, level, rewards, what it requires and where it
  sits in its chain, from the earlier quests to what it leads to next.
- `/beast` finds a beast hunters can tame, with its family and a portrait.
- `/achievement` shows an achievement.
- `/who` looks up a character by name and surname.
- `/price` shows an item's auction house prices, and `/crafter` finds guild members who can
  craft it.

The site has the same lookups: the [quests page](https://sidequest-guild.com/quests/) searches
quests by name, and the [tameable beasts page](https://sidequest-guild.com/beasts/) lists every
beast hunters can tame.

## Find a group in #lfg

Select a button in `#lfg` to spawn a voice channel for that kind of group. Fill in a name, a
note and who you need, then invite people or wait for others to join.

Select **PUG group** to bring in outside players who aren't in the guild. `#lfg` gives you an
invite link to share with them; anyone who joins through it sees only your group's channel and
`#sign-the-ledger`. The bot removes anyone who hasn't signed in or been confirmed within 30
minutes of the group ending, so tell them to sign in if they want to stick around.

## Class halls

Pick your class in `#choose-your-class` to join that class's post in `#class-halls`.
