# Find and join events

SideQuest plans raids, dungeons, leveling groups, PvP and social nights on the
[events page](https://sidequest-guild.com/events/) and in Discord's events forum. Sign up in
either place; both update the same sign-up.

## Find an event

1. Go to the [events page](https://sidequest-guild.com/events/).
2. Select a kind from the tabs (All, Raids, Dungeons, Leveling, PvP, Social, Transmog, Other) to
   narrow the list, or leave it on All.
3. Select an event to open it and see its roster and raid chart.

:::{figure} ../_static/shots/event-signup.webp
:alt: The event page's signup form, with character, class, role, status and comment fields.
:width: 100%

Signing up for a raid: pick your character, class and role, then your status.
:::

## Sign up on the site

1. [Sign in with Discord](join.md) if you haven't already.
2. Open the event.
3. Under **Your signup**, fill in your character, class and role (raids and dungeons only).
4. Choose your status.
5. Select **Save signup**.

:::{note}
A comment on your sign-up shows to other signed-in members and is repeated in the event's
Discord thread. Don't put anything in it you wouldn't say in guild chat.
:::

## Sign up in Discord

Every event has a post in the Discord events forum with three buttons:

- **Going**
- **Maybe**
- **Can't**

For a raid or dungeon, the bot asks for your character, class and role the first time you use
these buttons, then reuses them next time.

## Change or cancel your sign-up

1. Open the event on the site.
2. Change your status, or select **Withdraw** to remove your sign-up.

Changing your answer in Discord updates the site too, and the other way around.

## Get a calendar link

Add a private calendar link from your [account page](account.md) so new events show up in your
own calendar app without checking the site. See [Manage your account](account.md) for how.

## Mark away dates

If you know you'll miss some events, mark the dates on your [account page](account.md). Raid
leads see you're away on any event in that range, and you get the **Away on a Quest** Discord
role for those days.
