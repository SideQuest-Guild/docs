# Find and join events

SideQuest plans raids, dungeons, leveling groups, PvP and social nights on the
[events page](https://sidequest-guild.com/events/) and in Discord's events forum. Sign up in
either place; both update the same sign-up.

## Find an event

1. Go to the [events page](https://sidequest-guild.com/events/).
2. Select a kind from the tabs (All, Raids, Dungeons, Leveling, PvP, Social, Transmog, Other) to
   narrow the list, or leave it on All.
3. Select an event to open it and see its roster and raid chart.

:::{figure} ../_static/shots/event-signup.webp
:alt: The event page's signup form, with character, class, role, status and comment fields.
:width: 100%

Signing up for a raid: pick your character, class and role, then your status.
:::

## Sign up on the site

1. [Sign in with Discord](join.md) if you haven't already.
2. Open the event.
3. Under **Your signup**, fill in your character, class and role (raids and dungeons only).
4. Choose your status.
5. Select **Save signup**.

:::{note}
A comment on your sign-up shows to other signed-in members and is repeated in the event's
Discord thread. Don't put anything in it you wouldn't say in guild chat.
:::

## Sign up in Discord

Every event has a post in the Discord events forum with three buttons:

- **Going**
- **Maybe**
- **Can't**

Every Monday the weekly board posts a card for each of that week's events with the same three
buttons, plus **More info** (the event on the site) and **Thread** (its forum post). The card
lists who's going, who might make it and who can't, and catches up within about 15 seconds of
any change. For a raid or dungeon, Going is split into tanks, healers and DPS.

For a raid or dungeon, the bot asks for your character, class and role the first time you use
these buttons, then reuses them next time.

## Change or cancel your sign-up

1. Open the event on the site.
2. Change your status, or select **Withdraw** to remove your sign-up.

Changing your answer in Discord updates the site too, and the other way around.

## Get a calendar link

Add a private calendar link from your [account page](account.md) so new events show up in your
own calendar app without checking the site. See [Manage your account](account.md) for how.

## Mark away dates

If you know you'll miss some events, mark the dates on your [account page](account.md). Raid
leads see you're away on any event in that range, and you get the **Away on a Quest** Discord
role for those days.

## Recruitment drives

A recruitment drive is a 2 week guild event. Officers run one from time to time to bring in new
players, and it shows up on the events page like any other event, except there's nothing to
sign up for.

### How to invite

Invite someone the normal way, with a regular in-game guild invite. You don't need the addon to
take part.

### What counts

A recruit counts toward your total only if all of these are true:

- They're new to the guild. Someone who was already a member before the drive started doesn't
  count.
- They reach level 10.
- They stay in the guild for 7 days in a row. Quitting or getting removed during that week
  disqualifies the invite, though you can invite them again.
- They aren't your own alt. [Link Battle.net](account.md) on your account page so the site
  knows which characters are yours.

Whoever has the most recruits that count wins 1g. If two or more members tie for the most, each
of them wins 1g.

### Get paid

The winner gets the prize a week after the drive ends, by in-game mail from an officer.

### See the leaderboard

Open the drive's event page on the site, its thread in Discord, or the Events tab in the
SideQuest addon to see who's invited the most and who's qualified so far.

Each inviter has three numbers:

- **Qualified**: recruits who stayed 7 days and reached level 10. These decide the winner.
- **Potential**: recruits who joined and are still inside their 7 days. They count once their
  week is up, as long as they stay and reach level 10.
- **Invited**: everyone they invited during the drive.

The addon's copy updates when an officer's addon brings in new site data, so it can be a few
hours behind the site.
