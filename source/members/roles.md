# Roles and ranks

This page lists every Discord role SideQuest uses: the guild ranks, and the roles the site and
the bot hand out on their own. The same list, kept up to date by the bot, is in `#roles`, just
below `#addons`.

## Guild ranks

Officers give these roles by hand in Discord, and in game they promote you to the matching guild rank. Only
**New Arrival** comes on its own. Highest first:

| Rank | How you get it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Quest Master | A guild leader. | Full control of the site's officer tools: the roster, the activity log and moderation, on top of everything a Quest Guide can do. |
| Quest Guide | Picked by the Quest Masters. | An officer. Runs events, attendance and recruiting from the site. |
| Expedition Leader | Picked by the Quest Masters and Quest Guides. | Leads raids. |
| Seasoned Adventurer | Character level 60, and the {ref}`Raid Regular <hall-of-heroes-badges>` badge (10 raids with the guild). | |
| Adventurer | 14 days in the guild, and the {ref}`First Steps <hall-of-heroes-badges>` badge (came to a guild event). | |
| New Arrival | Automatic once you're confirmed on the site's roster: linking Battle.net and showing up on the in-game guild roster confirms you within about 30 minutes, or a Quest Master selects **Approve** on your sign-up notice, confirms you on the [Officers page](https://sidequest-guild.com/officers/), confirms you in the addon's Roster tab, or right-clicks you in Discord and selects **Confirm in guild**. | Opens the rest of the guild's Discord channels. See [Join the guild](join.md). |
| Alternate Path | Given by hand for an alt character. | |

See the [officers' code](../officers/conduct.md) and the
[guild rules](https://sidequest-guild.com/rules/) for how officers use these ranks.

## Other roles given by hand

| Role | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| NPC | Friends who don't play the game but want to be in the Discord. |
| Familiar | Automated or bot accounts that aren't Game Master itself. |

## Automatic roles

The site and the bot grant and remove these on their own.

| Role | How you get it |
|---|---|
| At the Gate | You're in the server but haven't signed in to the site and haven't been confirmed by a Quest Master. Removed once either happens. |
| Signed the Ledger | You signed in with Discord on the site. |
| Battle-Tagged | You linked Battle.net on your [account page](account.md). |
| New Arrival | See [Guild ranks](#guild-ranks) above. |
| Away on a Quest | You marked away dates on your [account page](account.md) that include today. |
| Level roles (Lv 5 · Wayfarer through Lv 50 · Legend) | Chat, voice, event and `#lfg` group XP levels you up; the matching role comes on its own. See [Discord channels and roles](discord.md#chat-levels). |
| Class roles | Pick your class in `#choose-your-class`; it's a self-select role menu, and picking one also adds you to that class's post in `#class-halls`. |
| PUG | An outside player who joined a [PUG group](discord.md) with its invite link. You see only that group's channel and `#sign-the-ledger`. Removed 30 minutes after the group ends, unless you sign the ledger or a Quest Master confirms you. |
| Server Booster | Discord's own role for anyone boosting the server. |

### Hall of Heroes badges

Each [Hall of Heroes](https://sidequest-guild.com/heroes/) badge is also a Discord role. The bot
grants it and announces it in chat the moment you earn it; a badge, once earned, is never taken
away, except Top Helper, which moves to the next month's winner. See the Hall of Heroes for the
exact rules and current standings:

First Steps, First Raid, Raid Regular, Raid Veteran, Perfect Month, Founding Member, Chatterbox,
Top Helper, Party Animal, Party Leader, Open Road, Crossroads Host and Caravan Master.

The last three are for hosting PUG groups from `#lfg`: a PUG group counts once outside players
spend ten minutes in it with the guild. Host your first for **Open Road**, 10 for **Crossroads
Host**, and 25 for **Caravan Master**.

::::{note}
A handful of roles aren't listed here because nothing about them is guild-specific: the bot's own
managed role (Game Master) and the deploy preview app's managed role (Cabotage Preview) are
Discord-managed and never granted or removed by hand.
::::
